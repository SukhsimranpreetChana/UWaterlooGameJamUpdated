Pixel SFX Free

Documentation. 30 sounds as WAV (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono), one AudioClip each, a folder
per kind:

  coins/ (6)
    coin_bright, coin_double, gem, gem_big, heart, key
  explosions/ (2)
    boom_big, explosion_01
  hits/ (3)
    death, enemy_hit, hurt
  jingles/ (2)
    game_over, level_complete
  magic/ (1)
    teleport
  movement/ (6)
    bounce, dash, jump_classic, jump_double, jump_high, land
  powerups/ (3)
    extra_life, heal, powerup_arp
  shooting/ (3)
    charge, laser_01, shot_classic
  ui/ (4)
    cancel, confirm, error, select

  Demo/PixelSFXFreeDemo.unity   a button per sound

Play one with AudioSource.PlayOneShot(clip). The WAVs import in any Unity version; the demo
scene needs Unity 6.3 or later.
